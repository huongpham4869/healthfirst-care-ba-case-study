## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the stakeholders relevant to the HealthFirst Care case study, summarises their concerns, assesses their influence and interest, and proposes engagement activities for requirements analysis and validation. The analysis is based on the simulated stakeholder profiles and case brief provided for this case study. It does not represent actual stakeholder interviews or completed engagement activities.

## 2. Stakeholder Identification

| Stakeholder | Role / Key Concern |
|---|---|
| Patients | Service recipients. Concerned with booking, waiting time and communication. |
| Doctors | Healthcare providers. Concerned with overbooked schedules, access to rooms and equipment, and timely information from other departments. |
| Nurses | Patient care and operational support. Concerned with staffing, availability of beds and equipment, and patient transfers. |
| Administrative Staff | Appointment scheduling, records and billing. Concerned with double bookings, doctor availability and record retrieval. |
| IT Team | Technical support for the current systems. Concerned with integration, network reliability and security. |
| Hospital Management | Sponsor and decision maker. Concerned with waiting times, resource bottlenecks, patient satisfaction and manual reporting. |

Hospital Management has no stakeholder profile in the case material; its concerns are taken from the case brief.

## 4. Influence and Interest Analysis

Stakeholders are assessed by their ability to influence project decisions and their level of interest in the proposed changes. The ratings inform the engagement approach.

| Stakeholder group | Role | Influence | Interest | Key needs | Category |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patients | Service recipients | Low | High | Easy booking, an assisted option, shorter waits, and timely notifications. | Keep informed |
| Doctors | Healthcare providers | High | High | Manageable schedules, available rooms and equipment, and timely information. | Manage closely |
| Nurses | Patient care and operational support | Low | High | Visibility of beds, rooms, and equipment; advance notice of patient transfers. | Keep informed |
| Administrative Staff | Scheduling, records and billing | Medium | High | Fewer double bookings, current doctor availability, and faster access to patient records. | Keep informed |
| IT Team | Technical support for current systems | High | High | Reliable online services, system integration, and secure patient information. | Manage closely |
| Hospital Management | Sponsor and decision maker | High | High | Reduced waiting times, better resource visibility, and current operational reporting. | Manage closely |

### 4.1 Stakeholder Matrix

|  | High Influence | Medium / Low Influence |
|---|---|---|
| **High Interest** | **Manage Closely**<br>Hospital Management, Doctors, IT Team | **Keep Informed**<br>Administrative Staff, Nurses, Patients |
| **Medium / Low Interest** | **Keep Satisfied**<br>(no stakeholder group in this quadrant) | **Monitor**<br>(no stakeholder group in this quadrant) |

The IT Team is rated High/High because it has a strong role in assessing technical constraints and system feasibility. Hospital Management retains approval of scope and priorities. Patients, Nurses, and Administrative Staff have high interest but less influence over project decisions, so their needs should be considered through consultation and process reviews.

## 5. Engagement Plan

| Stakeholder group | Engagement approach | Communication method | When input is needed | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patients | Collect feedback and validate patient-facing processes | Surveys; usability walkthrough | To-Be booking and notification review | Validate patient needs and usability |
| Doctors | Review and validate clinical workflow requirements | Workshops; meetings | Scheduling, resource and information reviews | Validate requirements and agree priorities |
| Nurses | Review check-in, resource allocation and coordination | Process walkthroughs; meetings | As-Is/To-Be process reviews | Validate process models |
| Administrative Staff | Review scheduling processes and requirements | Workshops; meetings | Process and requirements review | Validate scheduling requirements |
| IT Team | Review technical constraints and system capabilities | Meetings; email | Data, security and availability review | Confirm technical constraints |
| Hospital Management | Review and approve scope, priorities and requirements | Briefings; summary reports | Scope approval and final BRD review | Approve scope and requirements |
